# Media and prototype evidence

- `openbci-derived-headset-physical-prototype.png` and `openbci-derived-headset-print-bed.png` show the early 3D-printed mechanical frame. The frame is derived from the OpenBCI Ultracortex Mark IV; it is retained as history and is not a dependency for the current EMG/EOG build.
- `forearm-emg-electrode-setup.jpg` shows the surface-electrode arrangement during a forearm recording. It documents setup only; it does not prove signal quality or classification.
- `ten20-conductive-paste.png` and `disposable-gel-electrodes.png` document available consumables.
- The result plots are kept under `results/emg_initial_analysis/`.

Images are evidence of prototype work, not evidence of clinical readiness. Upstream headset design credit and license information are in the root README.
