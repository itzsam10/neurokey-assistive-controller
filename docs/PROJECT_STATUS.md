# Project status

## Stage 1: hardware and signal validation

The immediate objective is to verify the available hardware and obtain repeatable jaw EMG and eye-movement EOG recordings.

### Next evidence to publish

1. Photographs and names of the available boards and components
2. Photograph of the 3D-printed headset prototype
3. First jaw EMG waveform
4. First horizontal and vertical EOG waveforms
5. Notes describing electrode placement and recording conditions
6. Initial event-detection results

No detection accuracy or product-performance claim will be made until it has been measured over repeated trials.
